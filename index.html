ODD / EVEN

THE GAME

How it
works.

Each player starts with 10 marbles. Take turns hiding marbles and guessing odd or even.

01 /

Choose your marbles

Choose how many marbles to hide, or how many to wager when it's your turn to guess.

02 /
ODDorEVEN

Make your guess

Guess whether the other player is hiding an odd or even number of marbles.

03 /
↗

Collect all 20

A correct guess wins the wagered marbles. An incorrect guess costs you the wager. First to 20 wins.

GAME MODES
